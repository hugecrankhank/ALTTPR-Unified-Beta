More Kara settings

Settings, presets and help text (hover a setting) are from Kara's alttpr.gwaa.kiwi. Her generator (alttpr-python, MIT license, built on Aerinon's Door Randomizer and codemann8's Overworld Randomizer) runs here in your browser. The first seed loads Python (about 15 MB, kept for next time). Big shuffles can take a minute or more.

MSU-1 music
Game's own music

MSU-1: choose all the .pcm files of an ALttP MSU-1 pack at once (on iPhone, tap Select in the file picker, then select them all). Seeds you generate afterwards play the pack instead of the game's music, including seed files opened with Load ROM.

Generate a seed to start

Pick your settings in the Randomizer bar above, choose your own Japanese 1.0 ALttP ROM with Base ROM… (once), then press Generate & Play. Already have a seed .sfc? Use Load ROM… instead. The tracker on the right updates automatically as you play.

Everything runs in your browser. Your ROM is never uploaded.
